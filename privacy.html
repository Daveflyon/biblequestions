How we handle your information

What happens to what you send in.

What we collect

The text of the question or response you write and your email address. If you choose to, your name. Our spam check, Cloudflare Turnstile, also looks at your connection to decide whether you are a person.

Your email address is required and your name is optional. Please do not include personal details such as your address or phone number in what you write. A question can sometimes reveal your beliefs, so write only what you are comfortable sharing.

Why we collect it

To read your question or response and decide whether to publish it. To reply to you and to be able to trace what you sent if there is ever a question about it.

We read what you send under legitimate interests. We need to review it to decide whether to publish it or use it as a topic. We use your email address for the same reason. If you tick the box to show your name we use that with your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time. We do not check that an email address is real.

What is shown publicly

Nothing you send is shown until it has been read and approved. An approved response appears under its article. Your name appears with it only if you ticked the box to show it. Your email address is never shown.

A question you send is read as a possible topic for a future answer. It is not published as you wrote it.

Who holds it

The site and its database are provided by Cloudflare. Only the person who runs the site can read what is sent in.

How long we keep it

  • Responses and questions that are not approved are deleted after 12 months.
  • Items taken down after being published are deleted after 90 days.
  • Email addresses are erased 12 months after an item has been read and dealt with.

A short permanent record

So that we can answer a query later, we keep a brief record of each item for 6 years. It holds the date received, whether it was a question or a response, the article or topic, and what happened to it with the date. It does not hold what you wrote or your name. It holds a scrambled code made from your email address instead of the address itself. We can match the code only if you tell us the address you used. Because the code can still identify you, it is personal data, and we keep it under legitimate interests.

Your rights

You can ask to see what you sent. You can also ask to have it corrected or deleted, including your entry in the permanent record. Email hiturnmedia@gmail.com.

If you are unhappy with how your information is handled you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Who runs this site

The Open Question is a Hiturn Media Group resource. Hiturn Media Group decides how the information you send in is used. Contact: hiturnmedia@gmail.com.
